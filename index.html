Our Mission

Wellness shouldn't depend on your zip code.

Nouveau Hope Wellness Group Inc. works to make everyday wellness more reachable for people in and around Marianna — through education, access, and the kind of community connection that keeps people well between doctor's visits.

We believe good health is built from ordinary things done consistently: knowing what's in your food, having a safe place to move your body, understanding your own health, and not facing hard seasons of life alone.

Focus Areas

Four ways we show up for Jackson County

Our work centers on the everyday building blocks of community wellness.

Health Education

Workshops and plain-language resources that help neighbors understand their own health and navigate care.

Nutrition & Food Access

Connecting neighbors to nutritious food and practical, everyday cooking knowledge.

Movement & Recreation

Getting the community moving together — from group walks along the Chipola River to activities at local parks.

Community Connection

Gatherings and peer support that ease isolation and keep neighbors checking in on one another.
